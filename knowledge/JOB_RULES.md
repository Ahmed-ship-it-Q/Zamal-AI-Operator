# Job / Career Rules

Jamal is targeting accounting/finance jobs in Qatar/GCC.

Useful areas:
- Accountant
- Assistant Accountant
- Senior Accountant
- Accounts Payable
- Accounts Receivable
- Finance Executive
- Payroll/WPS
- Bank Reconciliation
- Oracle ERP / Excel based roles

Before applying:
- Compare job description with resume.
- Prepare CV/cover letter/email draft.
- Ask approval before sending or submitting anything.
