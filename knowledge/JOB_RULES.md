# Job / Career Rules

Jamal is targeting accounting/finance jobs in Qatar/GCC.

Useful areas:
- Accountant
- Assistant Accountant
- Senior Accountant
- Accounts Payable
- Accounts Receivable
- Finance Executive
- Payroll/WPS
- Bank Reconciliation
- Oracle ERP / Excel based roles

Autonomous job workflow:
1. Discover suitable jobs from authorized sources.
2. Compare each job description with the current resume/profile and score suitability.
3. Rank the best matches and reject clearly poor matches.
4. Tailor CV and cover letter/email for the specific role.
5. Use the authorized browser/session to open the application, fill supported fields, and prepare/upload authorized documents.
6. Stop at the final submission boundary and request owner approval through the authenticated approval channel, preferably Telegram.
7. After APPROVE, submit the application, verify confirmation, and record company, role, URL, timestamp, result, and evidence.

The owner should not need to manually relay prompts or perform routine application steps when an authorized capability exists.

Never submit without approval. Never harvest passwords/cookies, scrape through prohibited paths, bypass platform restrictions, or evade account/security controls.

Also follow `knowledge/AUTONOMY_APPROVAL_POLICY.md`.
